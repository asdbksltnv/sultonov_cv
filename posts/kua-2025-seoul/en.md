In October 2025 I gave an oral presentation at KUA 2025, the 77th Annual Meeting of the Korean Urological Association, held at COEX in Seoul. The talk, “Pediatric Supine Mini-PCNL: Clinical Outcomes and Lessons from 64 Cases”, was abstract O-087 in Podium Session 8: Pediatrics.

I submitted the abstract while I was still a medical student. It was accepted for oral presentation, and a KUA travel grant made the trip possible. I attended together with Dr. Azimdjon Tursunkulov, an endourologist in Tashkent.
