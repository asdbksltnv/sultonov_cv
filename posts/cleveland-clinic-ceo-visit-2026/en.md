On 20 October 2026 Dr. Tomislav Mihaljevic, CEO of Cleveland Clinic, made an official visit to Akfa Medline University Hospital. Cleveland Clinic is one of the leading medical centers in the world, so having its CEO at our hospital was a special day for all of us.

## A tour of the hospital

Dr. Mihaljevic toured the hospital and saw its medical infrastructure, its diagnostic and treatment capabilities, and how its work is organized to international standards.

## A conversation with doctors and residents

For me the most valuable part was the meeting with the hospital's doctors and residents. We talked about where modern medicine is heading, what can be learned from international experience, new approaches to care, and how medical education should develop.

Hearing these questions discussed by the head of a world-leading clinic gave me, as a young doctor, a clearer picture of what to aim for in my own training.

## Why it matters

Visits like this build international professional ties and open the door to sharing experience and future cooperation in healthcare. I am grateful to have been part of the conversation.
